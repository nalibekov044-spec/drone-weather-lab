# Drone Weather Lab 0.88 Beta

Three updates in this version.

1. Power. The battery uses a whole number of cells, from 1S to 16S. ESC limits follow the voltage drop under load. Flight time is now integrated until the reserve or the first power limit.
2. Air and CFD. Drag uses the air speed relative to the moving drone. CFD stops only after velocity, force, mass flow and density settle.
3. CAD. Pick the file units, set each size, lock proportions and rotate the model. Drag area can come from the body silhouette.

Download the ZIP, unzip it and open Drone-Weather-Lab-0.88-Beta.html. The model has not been checked against a real drone.
