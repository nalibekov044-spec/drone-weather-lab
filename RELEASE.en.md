# Drone Weather Lab 0.96.0 Beta

Wind at the flight height. The forecast gives the wind at 10 m, but the drone flies higher, where it blows harder. The weather section now has the flight height above the ground and the ground type. The wind comes from the forecast levels at 10, 80, 120 and 180 m, and pressure and temperature are lifted to that height too.

Fixed: after a forecast the pressure follows the altitude, and the forecast summary is shown with only the main settings as well.

Download the ZIP, unzip it and open Drone-Weather-Lab-0.96.0-Beta.html.
